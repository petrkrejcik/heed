# Pinning

`m` pins or unpins the selected task (mark, as in vim). Pinned tasks stay at the top of the sidebar whatever their
status, sorted by title so their numbers stay put, and `'1` to `'9` jump to them. Up to nine can be pinned.

A task you keep coming back to, such as Kibana main, is a good one to pin.

`''` goes back to the task you were on before.
